How to Read Villages Club Meeting Codes

In The Villages club lists, meeting schedules are encoded into shorthand at the end of each club name (e.g. 2,4W@3PM-LU). Our application automatically translates these into plain English, but here is how the codes work:

Q

Every Week

Q means Every week. For example, QF@6PM meets every Friday at 6:00 PM.

1, 2, 3, 4, 5

Week of the Month

Indicates which week(s) each month. 2,4W means the 2nd and 4th Wednesday. 1M means the 1st Monday.

M - SU

Day of the Week

M = Monday, TU = Tuesday, W = Wednesday, TH = Thursday, F = Friday, SA = Saturday, SU = Sunday.

@Time

Start Time

Indicates when the meeting begins, such as @9AM, @11:30AM, or @6PM.

-XX

Facility / Room Code

Abbreviation of the recreation center or room, such as -LU (Laurel Manor), -CC (Colony Cottage), or -LH (La Hacienda).

x / even / odd

Exceptions & Months

x7,8 means except July and August. 2Meven means 2nd Monday of even months only.
